Karnataka Culinary Specialties

From aromatic Mysore Pak and spicy Neer Dosa to traditional Bisi Bele Bath and Pandi Curry.

Mysore Masala Dosa

South KA

Crispy golden rice crepe smeared with spicy red garlic chutney and stuffed with seasoned potato masala, served with pure ghee.

Best in: Mysuru, Bengaluru

Neer Dosa & Mangalorean Curry

Coastal

Feather-light, lace-like rice crepes paired with rich coconut-infused coastal seafood curry or vegetable gassi.

Best in: Mangaluru, Udupi, Gokarna

Coorg Pandi Curry & Kadambuttu

Malnad

Iconic Kodava delicacy made with roasted dark spices and native wild garcinia (Kachampuli) vinegar, served with steamed rice dumplings.

Best in: Kodagu (Coorg), Madikeri

Jolada Rotti & Ennegai

North KA

Unleavened sorghum flatbread served with stuffed spicy brinjal curry, peanut chutney powder, and fresh butter.

Best in: Badami, Hampi, Hubballi
